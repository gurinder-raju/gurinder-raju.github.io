Aligning Priorities

### Why Is It So Hard?

Meet Lots Of Humans
===

### Practical Tip #1

Rule of thumb: meet at least one new human you haven't met before one-on-one every week.

```text +no_background
  week    mon    tue    wed    thu    fri
  ────    ───    ───    ───    ───    ───
   01             ●
   02                           ●
   03      ●
   04                    ●
   05                                  ●
  ──────────────────────────────────────────
  ● one-on-one with a human you haven't met
```

Reach out with curiosity. It's not a transaction. It's your curiosity to know them.

Ask About Goals
===

### Practical Tip #2

Ask humans about what goals they are focusing on. It's a great question. Whether the human on the other side is an SDM, Director, SVP, or the CEO.

```text +no_background
  "what goals are you focusing on?"

  ┌─────┐   ┌──────────┐   ┌─────┐   ┌─────┐
  │ SDM │   │ Director │   │ SVP │   │ CEO │
  └─────┘   └──────────┘   └─────┘   └─────┘
```

It sets a great stage for outcome focused forward looking conversations.

Align Goals
===

### Practical Tip #3

Align, Align, Align. Individual Goals, Team Goals, and Business Goals. Identify 2-fers.

```text +no_background
  ┌────────────────────┐       ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
  │                    │       ┃  2-fer                        ┃
  │  individual goals  │       ┃                               ┃
  │  team goals        │ ────▶ ┃  operational excellence       ┃
  │  business goals    │       ┃  + margin improvement         ┃
  │                    │       ┃  (shutting down a component)  ┃
  └────────────────────┘       ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛
```

Takes practice. But that's where the magic happens.

Segment Your Work
===

### Practical Tip #4

Develop deeper understanding of your time spend. Get a clearer picture of different segments:

```text +no_background
 ┌──────────────┬───────────┬───────────────────┬─────────────┐
 │ Team Reviews │ Reporting │ Artifact Creation │ Discussions │
 └──────┬───────┴─────┬─────┴─────────┬─────────┴──────┬──────┘
        └─────────────┴───────┬───────┴────────────────┘
                   ┌──────────┴──────────┐
          non-human segments       human segments
                   │                     │
                   ▼                     ▼
         agentification plan     keep your eye here
```

Develop a plan for agentification of the non-human segments over the next year. Don't take your eye off the human segments.

Use AI For Change Management
===

### Practical Tip #9

Humans are bad at processing changes and understanding the cascading implications. Feed status reports from your dependencies, your programs, and let AI process it. Don't ask what changed, but how does that impact you and your goals.

```text +no_background
 ┌ dependencies ┐   status    ╭──────╮
 ├ programs     ┼───────────▶ │  ai  │
 └ ...          ┘   reports   ╰──┬───╯
                                 │
        ✗  what changed?  ◀──────┤
        ✓  how does that  ◀──────┘
           impact me and my goals?
```

Open blind spots that can otherwise lead to late surprises.

<!-- end_slide -->

Use AI To Develop KPIs
===

### Practical Tip #10

KPI (Key Performance Indicators) help create objective direction for you and your dependencies. It uplevels the conversations from differing human opinions to measurable alignment.

```text +no_background
  differing human opinions            measurable alignment
  ┌──────────────────────┐           ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
  │ "I think..."         │  research ┃ KPI                  ┃
  │      "it feels..."   │ ─agents──▶┃ ▕█████████░░░▏       ┃
  │   "we believe..."    │           ┃ objective direction  ┃
  └──────────────────────┘           ┗━━━━━━━━━━┯━━━━━━━━━━━┛
                       ▲       iterate and tune │
                       └────────────────────────┘
```

Use more advanced research agents to develop KPIs for your most demanding programs. Iterate and tune.
